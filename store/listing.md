## English

**Title:** Dead Zone: Zombie Shooter

**Short description:**
Survive the dead city: shoot zombies, rescue people and build your shelter!

**Full description:**

The city fell in a single night. Mom is gone, your little brother Tyoma is missing, and the dead roam the streets. You have a knife, an old pistol and stubbornness. That's enough to start.

**DEAD ZONE** is a mobile zombie survival shooter with a story, humor and your own shelter.

🧟 **ZOMBIES OF EVERY KIND**
Slow walkers and fast runners, tank brutes, crawlers, spitters, exploders, screamers, armored bruisers and zombies in helmets. And bosses — the Drowned, the Shepherd and other monsters.

🔫 **WEAPONS AND ATTACHMENTS**
Knife, bat, axe, spear, pistol, shotgun, assault rifle, SMG, scoped sniper rifle and a flamethrower. Upgrade damage and magazines, add a silencer, red dot, laser and grip. Headshots pop heads!

📖 **A BIG STORY — 30 CHAPTERS**
Three parts: North-9, The Vaccine and South Port. Voiced cutscenes, funny and touching heroes: uncle Gosha and his coffee machine, Shnyr the accountant, doctor Irina and the Boar's gang.

🏕️ **YOUR OWN SHELTER**
Rescued people live by the campfire. Feed them, expand the yard from a camp to a fortress, build a garden, a watchtower, a radio station and a canteen. Add swings, a pool table, a barbecue and an open-air cinema. Happy residents earn coins!

🗺️ **LOCATIONS**
Streets, a container yard, a huge open city, a graveyard, a forest, an industrial zone, a seaport with a lighthouse and a snowy mountain pass. Day, dusk and night.

🚗 **CARS**
Drive and ram zombies. Car dealer, tuning, paint, neon, armor plates and spikes. Drifting and street races.

🛡️ **DEFENSE AND TRAPS**
Horde raids on your shelter: place turrets, bear traps and mines. Grenades and Molotov cocktails.

👥 **MULTIPLAYER**
Up to 4 players over Wi-Fi: co-op against zombies or against each other.

⭐ **AND MORE**
Daily rewards and quests, weekly events, 40 achievements, first- and third-person view, gyroscope aiming, customizable controls, runs on low-end phones.

Survive. Find Tyoma. Bring hope back to the city.
